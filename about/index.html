About YourTownPros

YourTownPros is a local business directory and matching service at yourtownpros.com. We list real local businesses, town by town and trade by trade, across all 50 states and Puerto Rico, so people can find a phone number, an address, hours and a rating without digging through ads first.

What a listing is

Every real business in the directory gets a listing with its name, address, phone number and public review summary. Listings are built from publicly available sources, and listing is free and automatic: a business does not have to do anything, or pay anything, to be listed. A listing is not an endorsement of a business.

Free to claim

The business a listing describes can claim it, free, at /claim/. Once we verify the claimant owns or manages the business, they can request updates to hours, description, services and photos. Every change is reviewed by a person before it goes live. Claiming is free and always will be.

Featured placement

Some pages show one Featured business at the top. Featured is a paid placement, it is always labeled Sponsored, and it never changes a rating, a review count or the order of the free listings below it. Claimed listings come first for matched customer requests; Featured businesses are flagged first in line. Details are on the For Business page at /for-business/.

Matching

Instead of calling around, a visitor can ask us to find a pro. We pass that request to local businesses that may respond. Those businesses are independent of YourTownPros; any agreement is between the customer and the business.

How we handle data

How listings are sourced is explained on /how-we-source-data/. Where ratings come from, and how they are labeled, is explained on /ratings-sources/. Our Privacy Policy is at /privacy/ and our Terms of Service at /terms/.

Contact

Questions, corrections and removal requests: see /contact/. Business owners can also start at /remove-or-claim-my-listing/.
