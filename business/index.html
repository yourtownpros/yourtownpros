What Featured includes

  • The Sponsored top spot on your town and trade page, labeled Sponsored.
  • A booking block on your listing, so customers can reach out in one tap.
  • First claim on matched customers in your town and trade: when someone asks us to find a pro, your business is offered first.

Featured is exclusive: one business per town and trade. It starts with a free taste week of routed demand, then a flat monthly lease for as long as you keep the spot. There is nothing to buy on this page; talk to us and we will set it up.
