Claim your business listing

Claiming is free. It tells us this listing is yours, so you can request changes and hear from us when customers ask for your kind of work in your town.

What claimed owners can do

Once we verify you own or manage the business, you can request updates to your hours, your description, your services list and your photos. Every change is reviewed by a person before it goes live, so listings stay accurate for everyone.

What claiming does not include

Featured placement at the top of your town, the online booking button, and first access to matched customers are separate paid options. Claiming your listing is free and always will be.

How verification works

To keep listings in the right hands, every claim includes one quick proof that the business is yours. When you pick your business below, the form shows the proofs that work for it: an automatic match when your account email is the email we already have for the business (or shares its website domain), a short code you put on your website, in your DNS, or on the business Facebook page, or a photo of a business license or utility bill emailed to us. Approval is automatic once our check finds your proof. Only when none of those work do we call the phone number on the listing as a last step.
