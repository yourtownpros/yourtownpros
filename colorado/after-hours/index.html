Who is actually open? Colorado's after-hours pros, counted

Most directories will tell you a business exists. Fewer will tell you whether it is open when you need it: Sunday morning, after dinner, at 2 AM with water coming through the ceiling.

We counted. YourTownPros carries 4,033 Colorado listings, and 2,591 of them publish hours we collected from the business's own website or its Google profile. Of those 2,591, 714 are open on Sunday, 225 stay open past 8 PM, and 54 are open 24 hours. Every count on this page comes from those published hours, listing by listing. The methodology is at the bottom, and the date matters: hours change, so this page carries the date the data was pulled.

Denver on its own: 142 listings, 68 with published hours. Of those, 24 are open on Sunday, 9 stay open past 8 PM, and 2 are open 24 hours: Denver Sewer and Water (plumbers) and Real deal towing & Recovery Llc.

Open 24 hours: 54 across Colorado

The 54 round-the-clock listings are concentrated where you would hope: towing, plumbing, and emergency trades, spread across the state rather than piled into Denver. The cities with the most: Rifle (5), Glenwood Springs (4), Grand Junction (4), Commerce City (3), Arvada (3), Colorado Springs (3), and Longmont (3). Denver has 2.

Methodology

Counts computed November 15, 2026 from the 4,033 Colorado listings published on YourTownPros. A listing counts as having hours only where the listing publishes them; hours on YourTownPros come from the business's own website or its Google profile, are recorded per day with their source, and are never templated or estimated. "Open on Sunday" means the listing publishes Sunday hours. "Open past 8 PM" means at least one day's published closing time is later than 8 PM (a midnight close counts as the end of that day). "Open 24 hours" means every day of the week is published as open around the clock. Listings whose hours we could not source are excluded from every rate on this page rather than guessed at: 1,442 Colorado listings publish no hours and appear in none of the open counts. Hours change; a business's own listing page carries its current published hours.
