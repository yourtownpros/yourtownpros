YourTownPros for Business

Customers in your town are searching for what you do. Here is what each level of YourTownPros includes.

Listed: free, automatic

Every real business in our directory gets a listing with its name, address, phone number, and public review summary. You do not have to do anything, and the listing is always free. Unclaimed listings cannot be edited, and matched customers are not sent to unclaimed listings first.

Claimed: free, verified

Claim your listing and, once we verify you own or manage the business, you can request updates to your hours, your description, your services list and your photos. Every change is reviewed by a person before it goes live, so the directory stays accurate. Claimed owners also hear from us when customers ask for their kind of work nearby.

How Featured works

Featured is the paid top spot on YourTownPros, built on top of a claimed listing. Claiming stays free either way; Featured is the upgrade. Here is exactly how it works.

  1. One spot per town and trade. Only one business holds the Featured spot for its kind of work in its town. While you hold it, no competitor in the same town and trade can take it.
  2. The top card, the badge, and booking. The holder gets the labeled Sponsored top card on its town and trade page, a Featured badge on its listing, and a booking block on the listing so customers can book or contact it directly.
  3. Matched customers come to you first. When a customer asks us to find a pro in your town and trade, that request is flagged Featured priority and recorded against your business, so you hear about the work before anyone else.
  4. It starts with a free taste week. The first week of routed demand is free, so you see real requests before paying anything.
  5. Then a flat monthly lease. After the taste week, Featured continues as a flat monthly lease for as long as you keep the spot, exclusive to your business in your town and trade.
  6. Counted from real data. Results are counted from real visitor data: views of your listing and its category page, and the matched requests sent your way. We never use estimates.

Matched customers

When a customer asks YourTownPros to find them a pro instead of calling around, that request becomes a matched customer. Matched requests are routed to Featured businesses first, so the Featured business in a town hears about the work before anyone else. If no Featured business covers that town and trade yet, the YourTownPros team handles the request and connects the customer with a local pro directly. Claiming your listing is free, and it is how we know who to contact about work nearby. Featured is what puts a business first in line.
