Water Heater Trouble: Repair It or Replace It?

From YourTownPros: YourTownPros is a directory of local home service companies. This guide walks through the repair or replace decision for a tank water heater in Denver, using the same prices and sources as our Denver water heater replacement cost guide. YourTownPros does not do this work itself and does not endorse any business listed.

The short answer

Repair the heater when the tank is sound and the fault is a replaceable part: Angi puts tank water heater repairs at $100 to $500. Replace it when the tank itself is leaking, or when the unit is past about ten years old, the average lifespan Angi reports, and repair bills are adding up. A typical like-for-like replacement runs $881 to $1,827 installed in Angi's 2026 national data, and Denver adds a $35 permit fee at a typical job value. Work through the decision in this order: the age of the tank, the symptom you are seeing, then the 50 percent rule on any quote you get.

Start with the age of the tank

Age decides more of these calls than the symptom does. Angi reports an average lifespan of about ten years for a tank water heater. Find the age on the rating plate on the side of the tank; the serial number on that plate usually encodes the month and year the unit was made. Then place your unit in one of these bands. The bands are a rule of thumb built around that ten year average, not a guarantee about your unit.

Age of the tankWhat it usually means
Under 6 yearsRepair first. The tank should have years of life left, so a repair in the $100 to $500 range is usually money well spent.
6 to 10 yearsIt depends. Repair a sound tank with a single part failure. Lean toward replacement if this is the second or third repair, or if the 50 percent rule below says the quote is too big for the life left in the tank.
Over 10 yearsReplace. The unit is past the average lifespan Angi reports, and money spent on repairs is usually better put toward a new tank with a fresh warranty.

Problems that usually mean repair

These faults live in parts a plumber can swap, not in the tank itself:

  • Thermostat: water runs too hot, too cold, or swings between the two, with no other symptoms.
  • Heating element, on electric models: water heats slowly or never gets past lukewarm.
  • Pilot light or ignition, on gas models: the pilot will not stay lit, or the burner will not fire.
  • Valves: a temperature and pressure relief valve that drips, or a drain valve that weeps.

On a sound tank, any of these is a repair job, and Angi's $100 to $500 range covers most of them. No hot water at all belongs on this list too, when the unit is young: it is usually one failed part, not a failed tank.

Problems that mean replace

These point at the tank itself, and a tank cannot be patched:

  • Leaks from the tank body: water pooling at the base, or rust streaks running down the side of the tank. The steel is failing, and there is no part to swap for it.
  • Rust-colored water: brown or rusty water from the hot taps while the cold water runs clear points to corrosion inside the tank.
  • Rumbling or popping: loud rumbling as the heater fires usually means sediment has built up on the tank floor, which makes the unit work harder and wears it out faster.
  • Repeated failures: a second or third repair in a short span, on a unit in the upper age bands, is the pattern the age rule warns about.

If the tank is leaking, the decision is already made: replace it, and treat it as urgent, because a leaking tank does not heal and the leak gets worse.

The 50 percent rule

The standard test compares the repair quote with the cost of a new installed unit: if the repair comes to more than half the replacement cost, replace instead. Run it with the Denver figures from our cost guide. A typical like-for-like tank replacement runs $881 to $1,827 installed, average $1,349, in Angi's 2026 national data. Half of the average is about $675, and half of even the low end of the range is about $440. Tank repairs run $100 to $500. So a $200 repair on a sound tank passes the test easily. A $500 quote on a ten year old unit fails it: that $500 is more than half the low end replacement cost, spent on a tank with little life left. Age breaks ties. The older the tank, the smaller a repair quote should be before you say yes.

If you replace: what happens in Denver

A permit is required. On the City and County of Denver's own permitting page, new or replacement water heaters at a single-family house or duplex are Quick permit work: no drawings, no plan review, a trades permit, and an inspection to check the finished work. The fee is $35 for work valued at $501 to $2,000, which covers a typical like-for-like swap, then $8 for each additional $1,000 of value. Your installer normally pulls the permit; a bid that quietly leaves it off is worth a direct question before you sign.

Pick the replacement type with the same numbers. A like-for-like tank swap is the cheapest route, at the $881 to $1,827 installed range above. Tankless runs $1,400 to $3,900 installed. A heat pump water heater costs more up front: Angi's national installed range is $1,200 to $3,500, while UniColorado, a Denver installer, quotes $6,900 to $8,000 installed in Denver before rebates, so budget a heat pump swap from local quotes, not from the national range.

The Xcel Energy rebate narrows the heat pump gap. Xcel pays its residential electric, gas, or combination customers in Colorado a $750 standard rebate plus a limited-time bonus rebate of $1,500, for up to $2,250 back, on an ENERGY STAR rated heat pump water heater with a minimum 3.3 UEF, listed on the AHRI Certified Directory, new and permanently installed. Units with storage over 80 gallons are not eligible, and indirect and commercial units are excluded. Xcel states no purchase-date requirement for the standard rebate. The bonus requires an invoice dated in 2026, and its funding is limited and first-come, first-served until the Public Utility Commission approved funding is expended. Apply by September 30 of the year after the equipment was installed. Colorado also offers a state heat pump tax credit of up to $250, with at least $83 of it reaching the homeowner, claimed through a registered contractor. The federal energy efficient home improvement credit for these units ended for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025, so do not budget for it on a 2026 install. Run your own numbers with the free Xcel water heater rebate calculator.

Before you sign with any installer, check the license. Plumbers are licensed at the state level in Colorado, and our Colorado license checker searches the recorded DORA licenses we hold by business name. Ask for written pricing that spells out the unit, the labor, the permit fees, and disposal of the old heater, so quotes can be compared line by line.

Call a pro today if

  • Water is actively leaking and spreading. Shut off the water supply to the heater if you can reach the valve safely, and call a plumber the same day.
  • You smell gas near a gas heater. Leave the house and call your gas utility or 911 from outside. Do not relight the pilot, and do not flip light switches on your way out.
  • You have no hot water at all, with a house full of people who need it. That is a same day call even on a young unit; the cause is often a single part.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a tank water heater last?

About ten years on average, in Angi's data. A unit under six years old is usually worth repairing when the tank is sound. Past ten years, replacement usually wins, especially when repairs start adding up.

Which water heater problems can be repaired?

Faults in replaceable parts: a thermostat, a heating element on an electric model, a pilot or ignition fault on a gas model, or a leaking relief or drain valve. Angi puts tank water heater repairs at $100 to $500.

Which problems mean the water heater has to be replaced?

A leak from the tank body itself, rust-colored hot water while the cold water runs clear, loud rumbling from sediment built up on the tank floor, and repeated failures on an older unit. A leaking tank cannot be repaired; it is replaced.

What is the 50 percent rule for water heater repair?

If a repair quote is more than half the cost of a new installed unit, replace instead. A typical like-for-like tank replacement runs $881 to $1,827 installed, average $1,349, in Angi's 2026 national data, so half the average is about $675. A $500 repair quote on a ten year old unit fails the test.

Do I need a permit to replace a water heater in Denver?

Yes. Denver treats replacement water heaters at single-family and duplex homes as Quick permit work: no drawings, no plan review, a trades permit, and an inspection of the finished work. The fee is $35 at the value of a typical swap, and your installer normally pulls the permit.

Is there a rebate if I switch to a heat pump water heater?

Yes, for Xcel Energy residential electric, gas, or combination customers in Colorado: a $750 standard rebate plus a $1,500 limited-time bonus, up to $2,250 back, on an ENERGY STAR rated heat pump water heater with a minimum 3.3 UEF listed on the AHRI Certified Directory. The bonus requires an invoice dated in 2026, and its funding is limited and first-come, first-served.
