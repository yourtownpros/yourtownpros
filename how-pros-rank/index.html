How pros rank on YourTownPros

YourTownPros is a local directory. When you search it for a local pro, we order the results so the most useful options come first. This page explains, in plain language, the principles behind that order.

Looking for a pro? Start a search from the homepage: pick a kind of work and a town, and the order described below is the order you will see.

What shapes the order

Six principles shape the free order. They work together, and no single one decides everything on its own.

Relevance. A pro has to match what you searched for: the kind of work, the services listed on the profile, and the town you asked about. A plumber does not rank for a hair salon search, however complete the profile is.

Distance. Closer pros generally come before farther ones, because most people hiring a local pro want someone nearby. Distance is judged from the town or area you searched.

Profile completeness. Profiles that carry more useful public detail, such as services, hours, photos, and a description, give customers more to judge by. More complete profiles can rank ahead of thin ones for the same kind of work in the same area.

Current information. Information goes stale: hours change, businesses move, phone numbers change. Profiles whose public information is current and consistent can rank ahead of profiles with old or conflicting details.

Customer fit. We look at how well a pro fits the specific request. For example, a pro who lists emergency callouts and same day availability fits an urgent request better than one who books weeks out, and a pro whose listed services match the job fits better than one who does not list them.

A rotating mix among close matches. When several eligible local pros fit a search about equally, we rotate which of them appear first over time, so different qualified pros get seen instead of the same few names taking every top spot week after week. Rotation only applies among pros that are a close match for your search.

Featured placement is paid, and always labeled

Some pros pay for Featured placement. Featured appears as a labeled Sponsored top card on its town and trade page, separate from the free order. It is always labeled "Sponsored · Featured" wherever it appears, so you can tell it apart from the free order at a glance.

Paying for Featured does not change a pro's place in the free order, and it does not change a rating, a review, or the facts on a listing. Ratings keep the label of the source they came from, and no business can pay for a better rating. You can weigh ratings yourself as you compare profiles: every rating on the site is labeled with its source.

The order can change

The order is not fixed. It can change as information and availability change: a pro updates hours, adds services, moves, or stops taking a kind of work, and the order adjusts. Two searches on different days, or from different towns, can honestly produce different orders. This page is an overview of the principles, not the full formula: some ranking inputs are proprietary, and we do not publish every detail.

If you own a business listed here

Owners can claim a listing, free, to manage its public details: request updates to hours, services, description, and photos, with every change reviewed by a person before it goes live. To claim, find your business at /claim/, sign in, and send the claim; we verify ownership before a claim is approved.

Keeping your claimed profile complete and current is the clearest step you can take, because completeness and current information are part of what shapes the free order. Business options, including Featured, are explained at /for-business/.
