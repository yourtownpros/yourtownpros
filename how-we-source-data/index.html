How We Source Data

Every fact on a YourTownPros listing comes from a source we can point to. This page explains where each kind of fact comes from and what we do when sources disagree.

Business names, addresses and phone numbers

Listings are built from publicly available sources: the business's own website, its public profiles, and public business records. A listing carries the business's real published phone number. We do not use tracking or call-forwarding numbers, and we never change a number to route calls through us.

Hours

Hours are taken only from the business's own website or its Google profile. Never from a template, never guessed, never copied from a similar business. Every set of hours we publish is logged with the source it came from. If neither source publishes hours, the listing shows no hours rather than invented ones.

If a source shows a business as closed, permanently or temporarily, we do not publish hours for it. Permanently closed businesses are removed from the directory. Temporarily closed businesses keep their listing without hours until a fresh check shows the business operating again.

Ratings and review counts

Ratings are labeled with the source they actually came from, Google or Birdeye, everywhere they appear: on the listing, in search results, and on town and trade pages. A Birdeye rating is never presented as a Google rating. The full explanation is on /ratings-sources/.

Reviews written on YourTownPros itself are a separate system. They are written by signed-in visitors, reviewed by a person before they appear, and never imported, seeded or bought.

Pins and locations

Map pins come from geocoding the listing's street address, using the US Census Bureau's public geocoder where an address is on file. A listing without a verified pin still appears in search, placed by its town until a pin is verified.

Corrections

Sources go stale. Businesses move, rename and change hours. If you own a listed business, the fastest correction route is to claim your listing at /claim/: verified owners can request changes, and every change is reviewed by a person before it goes live. Anyone can also write to us through /contact/. If information is wrong, we correct or remove it as appropriate, as our Privacy Policy at /privacy/ states.
