No Competitor Ads on Your Listing. Ever.

The promise

YourTownPros never sells advertising on your business listing to a competitor. Not now, not as a paid add-on, not ever.

What this means in practice

When someone opens your listing on YourTownPros, the page is about your business: your name, phone number, address, hours, photos, services, and reviews labeled by the platform they came from. The page also carries ordinary directory navigation, links back to your town page and your trade page, so a visitor can keep browsing the directory. What it never carries is a paid ad for another business. No rival's name, offer, or call button is sold space on the page your customers land on.

That is a real difference in how directories make money. Some large directories sell pay-per-click advertising, and those ads can appear on a business's own listing page, so the business ends up competing for attention on its own name. We chose not to build that product.

What we sell instead

We do sell one thing: Featured placement on our own category pages, the town and trade pages we publish, such as the plumbers page for a given town. A Featured business appears at the top of that page in a spot labeled Sponsored, ahead of the free listings. That space belongs to the category page, not to any business's listing, and buying it never places you inside another business's page.

Prices, exactly as published: Founding Monthly is $29 a month for the first 10 businesses, with the first week free, and it is not exclusive. Standard Featured is $49 a month. Founding Lifetime is $299 once, for the first 25, and it is exclusive: one business per city and trade. A Profile Build-Out is $99 once. Featured is placement only. It is not a promise of leads, calls, customers, or search rankings.

What claiming gives you

Your listing is free, and claiming it is free. No card is required. Claiming lets you correct your hours, phone number, address, and services, add real photos, and keep the page accurate as your business changes. Whether or not you ever buy Featured, your listing page stays yours: no competitor ad will appear on it.

This is policy, not a promotion

This page states a standing policy, not a launch offer with an end date. It applies to every listing on YourTownPros, claimed or not, paid or free. If this policy ever changed, the change would be announced on this page, with its effective date, before it took effect. Until you see that notice here, the promise above is the rule we operate under.
