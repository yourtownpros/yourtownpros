How does YourTownPros verify a contractor license?

We match each business to its DORA contractor record and print the license number, status, expiration date, and the exact entity name on the record, because a business sometimes holds its license under a slightly different legal name. You can check any Colorado contractor yourself with our free Colorado license checker or in DORA's own lookup.

What does an Active license status mean here?

Active is the status shown on the business's DORA record when we pulled it on November 27, 2026. In those records, every Active Plumbing Contractor license expires on February 28, so a status can change at renewal. A status other than Active is printed exactly as the state records it. Why does a business on this page show "State license not verified yet"?
