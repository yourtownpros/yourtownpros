Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026

Overview

YourTownPros is a local business directory and matching service operated at yourtownpros.com. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to this policy.

Information we collect

Listing data: business names, addresses, phone numbers, hours, ratings and review counts gathered from publicly available sources. This information is about businesses, not private individuals, wherever possible.

Information you give us: when you submit a match request or a claim request, we collect your name, phone number, email address and the details of your request. When accounts are offered and you create one, we collect your name and email address, and if you sign in with Google, the basic profile information Google shares with your permission.

Usage data: like most websites, we and our hosting providers receive standard technical data such as IP address, browser type and pages visited. We may use privacy-respecting analytics and cookies that are needed for the site to work and to understand aggregate usage.

When you visit our homepage, we may send your IP address to ipapi.co, a geolocation service, to estimate your city so the town box can be filled in for you. We do not store that estimate together with your name.

How we use information

We use the information to run the directory, to connect match requests with local businesses, to verify and process business claims, to respond to you, to improve the site, and to keep the service secure. We do not sell your personal information.

Sharing

When you submit a match request, we share your request details (name, phone, and what you need done) with one or more local businesses so they can respond. That is the service you asked for.

We use service providers for hosting, sign-in, database and email delivery, and they process information only to provide those services. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and users.

Business owners

If your business is listed and you believe information is wrong, you can claim your listing or email us and we will correct or remove information as appropriate. Claiming a listing may require verifying that you control the business phone number shown on the listing.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask for corrections, or ask for deletion by emailing yourtownpros@gmail.com. We respond to verified requests within a reasonable time. You can stop marketing messages at any time by telling us.

Data retention and security

We keep request and account information only as long as needed to run the service and meet legal obligations. We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information, but no system is perfectly secure.

Children

YourTownPros is intended for adults and businesses. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy and will post the new version on this page with a new effective date. Questions: yourtownpros@gmail.com.
