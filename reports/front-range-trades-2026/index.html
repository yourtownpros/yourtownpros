Front Range Trades Report 2026

From YourTownPros: YourTownPros is a directory of local home service companies. For this report we took the 500 most reviewed Front Range listings in five trades (plumbers, HVAC, electricians, roofing, and auto repair) and checked each one against records a homeowner can also check: the Colorado Department of Regulatory Agencies (DORA) license dataset, the Colorado Secretary of State business entity dataset, and the business's own website. Every figure below comes from those checks, run on November 27, 2026. YourTownPros does not perform trade work itself and does not endorse any business listed.

Prepared by YourTownPros from Colorado state records and the businesses' own websites, November 27, 2026.

Headline findings

  • Licenses verify by name for many, with the limit stated up front. Of the 105 plumbers and electricians checked, 29 of 61 plumbers (47.5 percent) and 34 of 44 electricians (77.3 percent) hold an Active contractor license in their own trade matched in state records. The rest are not verified by name, which never means unlicensed: the state records licenses under legal entity names, so Done Plumbing (recorded as Done Service Group LLC) and Flow Right (recorded under an FRPHI entity) both miss a name match.
  • These are established businesses. Across the 321 listings with a known age (of the 500 checked), the median age is 18 years; 236 (73.5 percent) are 10 or more years old and 114 (35.5 percent) are 25 or more years old.
  • More than a quarter have no website. 134 of the 500 (26.8 percent) have no website we could find, and 28 of the 366 websites on file (7.7 percent) were dead when checked.
  • Half match a state registration. 253 of the 500 (50.6 percent) matched a Colorado Secretary of State entity by name; 187 of those entities are in Good Standing. Not matching is a name matching result, not proof a business is unregistered.

1. License verification: plumbers and electricians

Colorado licenses plumbers and electricians at state level through DORA. HVAC, roofing, and auto repair are not state licensed trades in Colorado, so there is no state license to check for them; cities and counties license that work locally. We pulled the full DORA dataset on November 27, 2026: 1,620,991 records in total, of which 55,475 are unique business license records. The state holds 5,334 plumbing contractor records (3,124 recorded Active) and 12,432 electrical contractor records (4,769 recorded Active).

Within the 500 checked listings, the plumbers and electricians break down like this. A match means the listing's name matched a DORA business record; an active license means that record shows an Active contractor license in the listing's own trade.

4. Secretary of State registration

253 of the 500 listings (50.6 percent) matched a Colorado Secretary of State business entity at a name overlap score of 0.8 or higher. The other 247 produced no candidate at that threshold, which is a name matching result, not proof a business is unregistered.

In plain terms: Good Standing means the business's state filings are current; Delinquent means a filing is overdue; and a dissolved status means the entity was closed.

5. What we did not publish: Denver permit counts

We also queried the City and County of Denver permit records (ArcGIS layers 316 and 317) for the 71 Denver listings in the 500; 66 could be queried. We are not publishing permit counts from that query. The counts come from matching a single name token against the contractor name on permit records, and the token drives the result: a shop with the word Shop in its name records hundreds of hits that are not its permits, while a large firm whose permits are filed under a different name records zero. Publishing those numbers as permit activity would mislead, so the angle is dropped and the method is documented here instead.

Methodology

The sample. The 500 listings are the top 500 by Google review count from the Front Range population of 740 listings in plumbers, HVAC, electricians, roofing, and auto repair, taken from our listing snapshot of November 26, 2026. The mix is 228 HVAC, 140 auto repair, 61 plumbers, 44 electricians, and 27 roofing listings. Because the sample is the most reviewed slice, it describes established, visible businesses; a random slice of the directory would score worse on most of these measures.

Sources and pull dates. (1) Colorado DORA Professional and Occupational Licenses dataset, data.colorado.gov dataset 7s5z-vewr, pulled in full on November 27, 2026 (1,620,991 records; 55,475 unique business records). (2) Colorado Secretary of State Business Entities dataset, data.colorado.gov dataset 4ykn-tg5h, queried per business during the November 27, 2026 check. (3) Each business's own website, fetched November 27, 2026, with every fact we recorded carrying the source page and a verbatim quote. (4) City and County of Denver ArcGIS permit layers 316 and 317, queried November 27, 2026, and dropped as described above.

Statewide context. Across the whole Colorado directory, not just the Front Range 500, 293 listings matched a plumbing or electrical contractor record (252 on exact business name, 19 on a name prefix with the same city, 22 on a word set match with the same city). 252 of those 293 listings carry at least one Active license, and the 293 listings show 392 recorded licenses between them: 279 Active, 102 Expired, and 11 recorded as Need Master Hire - Cannot Practice. By directory category, 136 matched listings are electricians (124 with an Active license) and 107 are plumbers (93 with an Active license); 47 are HVAC companies that hold a plumbing or electrical license of their own.

Why license matches are published and permit counts are not. Permit records name individual permit holders and job addresses, so searching them by a contractor's name matches unrelated people who share part of the name. License records name the business itself, and our checker matched full business names in tiers, exact matches first, and shows the tier behind every match. That is why the license matches are published with their limits stated and the permit counts are not published.

Limits. License and registration matching is by business name; legal entity names, DBAs, and spelling differences all cause misses, and a miss is never treated as a negative finding. License status is printed exactly as the state records it, including statuses such as Expired and Need Master Hire - Cannot Practice. Website results describe a single visit on November 27, 2026; a site can be down for an hour or block automated visits while working normally for customers. Ages are computed as 2026 minus the stated or registered year.

Frequently asked questions

How many Front Range plumbers and electricians have a verified active Colorado license?

Of the 105 plumbers and electricians among the 500 listings we checked, 63 (60.0 percent) matched a Colorado DORA record showing an active contractor license in their own trade: 29 of 61 plumbers and 34 of 44 electricians. Another 7 matched a state record with no active license on it, and 35 did not match a state record by name at all.

Does no match in the state dataset mean a business is unlicensed?

No. Colorado records licenses under legal entity names, which often differ from the name a business trades under. Two well known Denver examples from our own checking are Done Plumbing, recorded as Done Service Group LLC, and Flow Right, recorded under an FRPHI entity. A name match can also miss for spelling and punctuation reasons. No match means we could not verify a license by name, nothing more.

How old are Front Range trade businesses?

For 321 of the 500 listings, an age could be established from the business's own founded statement or its state registration. The median age of those 321 is 18 years; 236 (73.5 percent) are 10 or more years old, and 114 (35.5 percent) are 25 or more years old. Among the 132 businesses that publish a founding year on their own website, the median age is 28 years.

How many top Front Range trade businesses have no working website?

134 of the 500 listings (26.8 percent) have no website we could find. Of the 366 listings that do carry a website, 312 (85.2 percent) loaded and served business content when checked on November 27, 2026, while 28 (7.7 percent) were dead, 14 (3.8 percent) were thin placeholder pages, and 12 (3.3 percent) blocked our check.

What does the Secretary of State registration check show?

253 of the 500 listings (50.6 percent) matched a Colorado Secretary of State business entity by name. Of those 253, 187 entities are recorded in Good Standing, 34 are Delinquent, and 22 are recorded as dissolved (18 voluntarily and 4 administratively). A dissolved entity record does not by itself prove a business has closed, because owners also dissolve entities when they reorganize or convert.
