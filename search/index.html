How results are ranked

Businesses in the town you searched come first, then nearest to farthest. When distances are equal, higher Google ratings and more reviews come first. One result may appear as a labeled Sponsored · Featured partner spot. That labeled spot is the only paid placement; nothing else about the order is for sale.

Map pins show listings with a verified street address. A listing without a verified address is placed by its town center for distance only and marked "Serves [Town] area"; it gets no map pin. Every listing keeps its town and category pages.
