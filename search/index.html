How results are ranked

Featured partners come first and are labeled Sponsored · Featured. After them, pros with reviews come before pros without reviews: among reviewed pros, a higher rating counts for more, and so do more reviews. Pros without reviews follow, nearest first. The Sort control in the filters can reorder the list by rating, review count, or distance instead. The labeled Featured spot is the only paid placement; nothing else about the order is for sale.

Map pins show listings with a verified street address. A listing without a verified address is placed by its town center for distance only and marked "Serves [Town] area"; it gets no map pin. Every listing keeps its town and category pages.
