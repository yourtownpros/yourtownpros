Frozen and Burst Pipe Plumbers in the Denver Metro

From YourTownPros: YourTownPros is a directory of local home service companies. It publishes these seasonal pages from records it checks itself, so Denver metro homeowners can find help fast in winter. YourTownPros does not do this work itself and does not endorse any business listed.

A frozen pipe is a clock. Water expands as it freezes, and a pipe that splits overnight can flood a basement by morning. This page lists nine Denver metro plumbing companies we checked on November 27, 2026, the day this page was built. For each one we print only what we could verify: a Colorado license record, hours read from the company's own website, or a Google rating from its listing. Where we could not verify hours, the listing says so in plain words.

What we verified

Three of the nine companies publish hours we verified from their own websites: Applewood in Denver (6 AM to 10 PM, seven days a week), Done in Aurora (7 AM to 7 PM on weekdays, 8 AM to 5 PM on weekends), and Mesa in Boulder (7 AM to 10 PM daily). Seven of the nine hold an active Colorado plumbing contractor license that we matched in Colorado DORA records on November 27, 2026. One company, Plumbline in Arvada, publishes a 24/7 availability statement on its own Arvada page and names burst pipes as an emergency it responds to; we quote that statement as the company's own words rather than calling it hours. The other five publish no hours we could verify, and their listings below say so. No company on this page paid to be here, and the order follows what we could verify, not ratings.

First moves when a pipe bursts

Shut off the main water valve first; every minute of flow is more water in the walls and floors. Open the faucets to drain the lines, and keep people and pets clear of electrical outlets or appliances near standing water. If a pipe is frozen but has not burst yet, open the faucet it feeds and warm the frozen section gently with a hair dryer or a space heater. Never use a torch or an open flame. Then call a plumber from the list below.
