No-Heat Furnace Repair in the Denver Metro

From YourTownPros: YourTownPros is a directory of local home service companies. It publishes these seasonal pages from records it checks itself, so Denver metro homeowners can find help fast in winter. YourTownPros does not do this work itself and does not endorse any business listed.

A furnace that quits in a Denver cold snap is not a next-week problem. This page lists ten Denver metro heating companies we checked on November 27, 2026, the day this page was built. Five publish hours we verified from their own websites. Six hold a Colorado contractor license we matched in DORA records; because Colorado does not license HVAC work at the state level, those licenses are plumbing or electrical contractor licenses, and each listing names which kind it is. Where we could not verify hours, the listing says so.

What we verified

Five of the ten publish hours that survived our verification protocol: Brothers in Denver (office hours 6 AM to 8 PM on weekdays and 7 AM to 8 PM on weekends), Heart in Denver (7 AM to 6 PM, seven days a week), Golden West in Denver (office hours 7 AM to 5:30 PM on weekdays), Elkhorn in Littleton (office hours 7:30 AM to 5 PM on weekdays), and Welzig in Longmont (8 AM to 5 PM Monday to Saturday, Sunday closed). Golden West, Elkhorn, and Welzig also publish a separate 24/7 emergency service line on their own sites; we print that as the company's own statement, not as office hours. The other five publish no hours we could verify. One more finding worth printing: Welzig's DORA plumbing contractor record shows Expired as of February 28, 2023, so this page prints no active state license for it.

Before you call

Check the thermostat, the furnace switch, the breaker, and the filter first; a clogged filter stops more furnaces than any broken part. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. Do not relight a pilot or open a furnace cabinet if you are not sure what you are doing.

How the license facts on this page work

Frequently asked questions

My furnace stopped working. What should I check before I call?

Check that the thermostat is set to heat and turned up, that the furnace switch and breaker are on, and that the filter is not clogged. If you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or your gas utility from outside. If those checks do not fix it, call a heating company; five companies on this page publish verified hours.

Which Denver metro heating companies are open on weekends?

Brothers publishes verified weekend office hours, Saturday and Sunday 7 AM to 8 PM, and Heart publishes hours seven days a week, 7 AM to 6 PM. Golden West, Elkhorn, and Welzig publish a separate 24/7 emergency service line on their own websites. Welzig's verified regular hours run Monday to Saturday, with Sunday closed.

Are HVAC companies licensed by the state of Colorado?

No. Colorado runs no state licensing program for heating and cooling work; cities and counties license the trade locally. Some companies on this page also hold a Colorado plumbing or electrical contractor license, and each listing names the license type and number exactly as DORA records it. Never read a plumbing or electrical license on this page as an HVAC license.

Why do some listings say hours not verified?

Because the company publishes no opening hours on its own website that our two independent reads could confirm on November 27, 2026. We print hours only when both reads agree on the same hours from the company's own site.
