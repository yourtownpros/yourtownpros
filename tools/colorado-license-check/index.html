A note on heating, cooling, and roofing

Colorado does not license these trades at the state level

Plumbers and electricians are licensed by the state, through DORA. Heating and air conditioning work is not licensed by the state in Colorado, and neither is roofing: DORA itself names roofers and general contractors as occupations the state does not license. Cities and counties run their own licensing for those trades, so for an HVAC or roofing company the building department where the work will be done is the record that counts. That is why this checker has no state record to show for them, and why it will never invent one.

Questions, answered plainly

Where does the license information in this checker come from?

From license records published by the Colorado Department of Regulatory Agencies, known as DORA. YourTownPros records the status, license number, and expiration date for businesses in our directory, and this checker shows what we recorded on the snapshot date printed on this page. It is a copy, not a live connection to the state database.

What does it mean if a business is not found in this checker?

It means the business is not in the snapshot this checker carries, or its license record has not been recorded by YourTownPros yet. It does not mean the business is unlicensed. Use the official DORA license lookup linked on this page to check the live record for any Colorado contractor.

Is the status shown here the current status?

Not necessarily. A license status can change after the snapshot date, for example when a license is renewed, expires, or is acted on by the state. Treat this checker as a starting point and confirm the live record in the official DORA lookup before you hire.

Which trades does Colorado license through DORA?

Plumbers and electricians are licensed at the state level in Colorado, including plumbing contractors and electrical contractors. Heating and air conditioning work is not licensed by the state in Colorado, and neither is roofing: DORA itself names roofers and general contractors as occupations the state does not license. Cities and counties run their own licensing for those trades, so check with the building department where the work will be done. This checker covers the state records we have recorded, and it never shows a state record for a trade the state does not license.
