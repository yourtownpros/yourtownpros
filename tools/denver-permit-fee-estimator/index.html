Questions, answered plainly

How does Denver calculate a building permit fee?

From the valuation of the work, using the fee table on the city's Building and Land Development Fees page. Small jobs pay a flat fee, and larger jobs pay a base amount plus a charge for each additional $1,000 of valuation or fraction of one.

Is the plan review fee included?

Denver lists a plan review fee of 50 percent of the permit fee for valuations above $2,000, and no plan review fee at $2,000 or less. Quick permits, which cover like-for-like replacements such as water heaters and furnaces, have no plan review fee.

Does this estimate include every city charge?

No. It covers the building permit fee and the standard plan review fee from the city's table. Denver also charges a separate zoning construction permit fee on many projects, plus charges such as reinspection fees when they apply. The city sets the final amount.

What counts as the valuation of the work?

Denver's Admin 138 policy defines valuation as the total value of all construction work for the permit, including labor, materials, profit, overhead, and permanent equipment. If the city's valuation is higher than the one on the application, fees are assessed on the higher number.
