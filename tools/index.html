Which trades does Colorado license at the state level?

Plumbers and electricians, including plumbing contractors and electrical contractors, are licensed by the state through DORA, the Colorado Department of Regulatory Agencies. Heating and air conditioning work and roofing are not licensed by the state in Colorado. Cities and counties run their own licensing for those trades, so the building department where the work happens is the record that counts for them. The license checker says this on its own page too, and it never shows a state record for a trade the state does not license.
