Questions, answered plainly

Why are there so few results?

This finder only shows businesses whose hours we verified from the business's own website. If a business does not publish hours, or we have not verified them yet, it does not appear here, even if it is open. The full category pages list every business.

What does open now mean here?

It means the current time in Denver falls inside the hours the business publishes on its own website, or the business publishes a 24/7 emergency service claim on its own site. It is not a guarantee that a crew can come out right away.

Where do the hours come from?

From each business's own website, checked on November 27, 2026 for this sample. Every result shows the hours exactly as published and links to the page they came from.

A business near me is open but not listed. Why?

Its hours may not be published on its own website, or our verification pass has not reached it yet. You can still find it on the full category pages linked below.

How this sample was built. Each business's hours were read from its own website on November 27, 2026, then re-read independently, and disagreements were adjudicated against the site before any hours were used. Availability claims that a site makes only about answering calls, or hours that appear only in page metadata with no visible hours text, were not counted. This sample grows as more businesses have their hours verified.
