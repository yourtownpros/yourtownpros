Questions, answered plainly

Who can get an Xcel Energy heat pump rebate in Colorado?

Xcel says residential electric and natural gas customers in Colorado are eligible to receive heat pump rebates. Xcel also says income-qualified customers may be eligible for additional rebates and services covering up to the full cost of a heat pump and its installation, and Xcel sets the income limits for that help.

Why does this checker ask me for the rebate rate instead of showing it?

Xcel publishes its current dollar amounts in its Rebate Summary Sheet, linked from its Heat Pumps page, and it can change those amounts. We link you to that sheet and do the math on the rate you read there, so you are always working from the rate Xcel is publishing now, not a copy that could go stale.

What are the deadlines?

Xcel says invoices and installations must be completed by December 31, 2026, and rebate applications will be accepted through September 30, 2027.

Can the rebate be more than my project cost?

No. Xcel says rebate amounts vary based on qualifying equipment and cannot exceed installation costs. This checker applies that cap to every estimate.

Does Xcel decide my rebate, or does this page?

Xcel decides. This checker applies the rules and deadlines Xcel publishes on its Heat Pumps page to your answers. If anything here disagrees with Xcel's page, Xcel's page wins.
