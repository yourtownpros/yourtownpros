The terms, up front

What Xcel's page says

So the result is never a surprise, here are the terms this calculator applies, in Xcel's own substance:

  • Who: Xcel Energy residential electric, gas, or combination customers in Colorado.
  • What: replacing a water heater with a qualifying heat pump water heater: ENERGY STAR rated, minimum 3.3 UEF, listed on the AHRI Certified Directory. Indirect and commercial units are excluded.
  • How much: a standard rebate of $750, plus a limited-time bonus of $1,500, up to $2,250 in total.
  • When: no purchase-date requirement for the standard rebate. Apply by September 30 of the year after the equipment is installed.
  • Size limit: units over 80 gallons are not eligible.
  • The bonus: requires an invoice dated in 2026. Its funding is limited and first-come, first-served until the Public Utility Commission approved funding is expended.

Source: Xcel Energy, Water Heaters. If anything on this page ever differs from that page, that page is right.

What about the federal tax credit or the Colorado HEAR rebate?

Both have ended: the federal energy efficient home improvement credit (Section 25C) ended for equipment placed in service after December 31, 2025, and Colorado's HEAR rebate program has ended as well. For a qualifying heat pump water heater, the Xcel rebate estimated above is the remaining incentive.

Questions, answered plainly

How much is the Xcel Energy water heater rebate in Colorado?

For Xcel Energy residential electric, gas, or combination customers in Colorado, Xcel lists a standard rebate of $750 for a qualifying heat pump water heater, plus a limited-time bonus of $1,500, for up to $2,250 in total. The bonus requires an invoice dated in 2026, and its funding is limited and first-come, first-served.

Who qualifies for the rebate?

Based on the terms Xcel publishes: you must be an Xcel Energy residential electric, gas, or combination customer in Colorado; the equipment must be an ENERGY STAR rated heat pump water heater with a minimum 3.3 UEF, listed on the AHRI Certified Directory; and the unit must be 80 gallons or smaller, since units over 80 gallons are not eligible. Apply by September 30 of the year after the equipment was installed.

Does this calculator decide my rebate?

No. This calculator applies the terms on the Xcel Energy water heaters page to the answers you enter, in this page only. Xcel makes the final decision on every rebate, and its page is the source to trust if anything here differs.
